Folio — Privacy

Folio is a personal book reader, used by its owner and the people they invite. Last updated 11 October 2026.

Without an account

Your books, reading progress, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device, in the browser's storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.

With sync (Sign in with Google)

  • What we receive from Google: your email address and a Google account identifier, to know which library is yours.
  • What is stored on Folio's server (Cloudflare): your email address; your reading data (place in each book, bookmarks and notes, series, "where I stopped", settings, reading time); book titles, sizes and small cover pictures; and, for each device, a random sign-in token (only its fingerprint is kept).
  • Your book files are not stored on Folio's server. They stay on your devices and, if you turn it on, in your own Google Drive.

Google Drive

  • When you turn on Library in Google Drive, Folio asks Google for access to your Drive, so it can see the books you put in its Folio folder yourself (from a computer, a phone or the Drive app).
  • Folio only reads, adds and removes book files inside that Folio folder and the folders in it. It does not open, read, change or delete anything else in your Drive.
  • Files travel directly between your device and Google. Folio's server keeps the Drive permission encrypted, and uses it only to give your own devices short-lived access, and to stream a book to family members you chose to share your library with.
  • You can disconnect Drive in Folio's Settings at any time, or remove Folio's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Folio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Translation and read-aloud

When you translate a word or have text read aloud, that text is sent to Google Translate or Microsoft's speech service to get the result. Nothing else about you is sent with it.

What Folio never does

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, and your data is never sold or shared with anyone, except the book files you yourself share with family through the family library.

Removing your data

Signing out in Settings removes that device's access. To have your account and all its reading data deleted from Folio's server, ask the person who gave you Folio; it is done in full.
